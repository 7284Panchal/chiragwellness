Questions, answered

Do I need a genetic test to start?

No. Genomic insights are optional. We can begin with your health history, lifestyle and goals, and add genetic information later if it makes sense for you.

Is this a replacement for my doctor?

No. Coaching supports your wellness and nutrition habits and does not diagnose or treat medical conditions. If you have a health condition or take medication, we work alongside your doctor.

How soon will I see results?

Everyone is different. Many clients notice better energy and routines within a few weeks, while body changes depend on your goals, consistency and starting point. Results are not guaranteed.

Are sessions online?

Yes. Sessions run by video call with chat support, so you can work with me from anywhere.

What happens on the free call?

We spend about 30 minutes on your goals and challenges. You leave with a clear idea of next steps, whether or not we work together.
